# remesch.com
#
# Two categories, treated differently.
#
# Training crawlers harvest pages into datasets that models are trained on.
# They get nothing, anywhere.
#
# Search assistants fetch a page to answer a question and cite it, which sends
# a reader back here — the same bargain as an ordinary search engine. They may
# read the writing. They may not touch the photographs.
#
# The photographs themselves are served from remesch-photos.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com,
# which is a different origin and needs its own robots.txt. There is one there.
#
# All of this is voluntary. It binds crawlers that choose to honour it and does
# nothing to a scraper that doesn't, and it cannot reach anything already taken.
